My Current Project

Fun Fact: Im currently trying to build the worlds fastest Windows 98 SE laptop

It is built from a 2 laptops, a Dell Inspiron 9200 and 9300, 9200 has the GPU I needed to harvest but the 9300 has the updated chipset

Specs;

2.3GHz Intel Pentium M 780 (last x86 CPU made by intel and their fastest)
ATI Radeon Mobility 9700 Pro 256MB (2nd fastest laptop GPU with 98 SE support behind the 9800, sadly 9800 use Pentium 4's which are worse)
512MB of Laptop DDR2 533mhz
1920x1200 60Hz LCD panel (crazy for 04')

Sadly I had to frankenstein 2 similar platforms together to do this, the 9200 only supports upto a 1.7ghz Pentium M and 333mhz RAM, the expanded bus speed on its successor makes it a lot faster. Regardless its really fast for 98 considering 98 mostly ran on 300mhz CPU's with 64-128mb of ram and 32mb of vram if you had a proper graphics card.
